# Privacy Policy for Void Player

**Last Updated: September 8, 2026**

**Byte-Labs** ("we", "us", or "our") developed the **Void Player** application ("Application") as a free, high-fidelity music player. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Application on Windows and other supported platforms.

---

## 1. Summary: Zero Personal Data Collection

**Void Player does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.** 
We believe in absolute user privacy. All playback, library scanning, audio processing, and AI categorization happen **100% locally and offline on your device**.

---

## 2. Information We Access and How It Is Used

To provide its core music player functionality, the Application requests and uses the following local capabilities:

- **Local Music Library Access (`musicLibrary` / File System)**:
  - **Purpose**: The Application reads audio files (such as MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, AAC, and OGG) from folders you explicitly select or from your system's default Music folder.
  - **Data Handling**: Audio file tags (artist, album, title, lyrics, and embedded artwork) are read strictly on-device to build your local library view. **None of your music files, metadata, or browsing habits are ever uploaded to any server or external service.**

- **Local Cache and Configuration Storage**:
  - **Purpose**: Cached album art and user preferences (such as playback speed, volume, equalizer presets, and playlist queues) are stored in your local application data folder (`%LocalAppData%\VoidPlayer`).
  - **Data Handling**: This data remains exclusively on your local machine and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the Application or resetting app data.

---

## 3. On-Device AI & Audio Intelligence Engine

Void Player features an on-device Small Machine Learning (SML) engine for smart categorization and AI DJ transitions:
- All acoustic feature extraction, tempo estimation, and genre classification are executed **entirely on your local CPU without internet connectivity**.
- No audio fingerprints, playback history, or listening profiles are transmitted to Byte-Labs or any third party.

---

## 4. Internet Access & Network Transmission

- Void Player does **not** transmit telemetry, tracking cookies, analytics, or behavioral data over the internet.
- The Application functions with full capability completely offline.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

- The Application contains **no third-party advertising SDKs**, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics frameworks.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade your information with any third parties.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Because Void Player does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), in compliance with COPPA and GDPR.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Since the Application operates offline without collecting contact information, we encourage you to review this policy periodically.
